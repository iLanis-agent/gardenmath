🌱 GardenMath

Garden bed math that holds up. Spacing, seeds, succession, yield - numbers you can plant on.

Plants per bed

A 4x8 bed is not "a few plants" - at the right spacing it is a couple hundred carrots or exactly 32 tomatoes' worth of ambition (tomatoes want more room; read on).

Seed packet honesty

Packet germination rates are for fresh seed in a lab. Your drawer-aged packet and real soil take their share first.

Succession plantings

One sowing is one harvest. Staggered sowings turn a season into a conveyor belt - if the window fits them.

Yield honesty

Seed catalogs photograph the best plant of the best year. Plan the pantry on a reality factor, not the photo.

Why these numbers

The assumptions, stated honestly
ConstantValueWhy
Square-foot densities1, 4, 9, 16 per sq ftMel Bartholomew's square-foot gardening standard, from 12/6/4/3-inch spacing on 144 sq in
Intensive vs rows36-inch rows are for tractorsRow spacing serves wheel hoes and machines, not a raised bed you reach into
Germination decayabout 15% per yearSeed in a drawer loses vigor roughly geometrically; cool and dry slows it, warmth kills it
Survival to harvest80%Cutworms, thinning, and weather take their share between sprout and plate
Reality factor0.5-0.9 of catalog yieldCatalogs quote the best plant of the best year; a real bed averages lower

Soil, sun, water and your climate move every number here. The bed tells you the truth by July.
